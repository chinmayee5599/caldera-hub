Guide

Every feature, and how to turn it on

A Caldera page can show a lot more than a profile: your daily journal, your repos as a living city, live visitors parachuting in, your AI agents flying over the code they're working on, your AI usage, your story with links, and a private view only you see. Here is every feature, what feeds it, and how to turn it on. Most take one prompt pasted into Claude Code, Codex or Cursor. New here? Start with the setup prompt: it covers all of this, asks before anything goes public, and is safe to paste again.

Your page

Masthead and photo

What feeds it: "banner" and "photo" in Caldera/profile.md (images inside the vault)

Turn it on: Choose images in Private view, or copy the masthead prompt for your AI.

Elsewhere (links)

What feeds it: "links" in Caldera/profile.md (Label | https://url)

Turn it on: Your site, GitHub, socials.

Now

What feeds it: "now" in Caldera/profile.md (3 lines)

Turn it on: Write it yourself or let your AI propose it (you approve).

Topics

What feeds it: tags you allow-list in Settings -> Caldera -> Public topics

Turn it on: Only allow-listed tags are ever counted, so private names never appear.

Side menu groups

What feeds it: "sidebar" in Caldera/profile.md (Group | item, item | optional vault folder)

Turn it on: Hand-pick companies, projects or sources to list; items named in your timeline or projects link to their sites.

Local graph and backlinks

What feeds it: "graph" (note names) and "backlinks" (Title | context) in Caldera/profile.md

Turn it on: Pick names you're comfortable showing; the page shows how many notes link to each.

Your story and your work

Timeline with eras and links

What feeds it: "timeline" in Caldera/profile.md: "Year | milestone | detail" lines, chapters as "era | Name | 2015-2020", links as [[Name|https://...]]

Turn it on: Only things already public; linked names also become links in the side menu.

Prompt for your AI
caldera-story.md
Help me turn on a timeline and projects with links on my Caldera page (https://caldera.social/@<my handle>/). Caldera builds that page from my Obsidian vault: <ABSOLUTE PATH TO MY OBSIDIAN VAULT>

Timeline and projects: my story and live work, each linking to the real thing.
1. In "<ABSOLUTE PATH TO MY OBSIDIAN VAULT>/Caldera/profile.md", propose "timeline" lines from things already public about me: "Year | milestone | one-line detail", chapters as "era | Name | 2015-2020", and links as [[Name|https://...]] (linked names also become links in my side menu).
2. Propose "projects" lines for my public projects: "Name | status | https://live-url".
3. Optionally propose "sidebar" groups ("Companies | A, B, C | optional/vault/folder") and "knows" lines ("Label | vault folder", counts only).
Show me everything first; write only what I approve. The page republishes about a minute after profile.md changes.

Rules:
Everything public is on the open internet, can be copied forever, and can affect other people. Be very cautious. Never write any of these into anything public:
- Other people: no names, initials, handles, photos or descriptions that identify anyone I meet, work with, hire, date, advise or invest with. Say "a founder", "the team", "a friend" only when it adds nothing identifying - usually leave it out.
- Meetings: no meetings, calls, lunches, dinners, interviews or events - not their titles, times, dates, attendees, or that they happened with someone specific.
- Times and places: no times of day, schedules, travel, cities, venues, addresses, offices, home or "where I am".
- Secret or sensitive work: no unannounced, stealth, confidential or NDA projects, codenames, deals, fundraising, investors, acquisitions, valuations, hiring, firing or anything said in confidence.
- Things that can get someone in trouble: legal, medical, health, HR, money, salaries, debts, relationships, family, children, politics, religion.
- Proprietary methods: no trading strategies, signals, indicators, thresholds, regime or risk rules, entry/exit logic, win rates, model weights, prompts, data sources or other trade secrets - in the journal, my notes or anywhere public. Name the theme only ("worked on the trading system").
- Contact details and links: no emails, phone numbers or links to private documents. No money amounts at all.
Describe work only by its public theme ("shipped a release", "wrote about design") and only name things I have already made public on my page (my own public projects, topics and links). If you are unsure, leave it out - an empty field is fine. Caldera also drops any journal line that looks sensitive before publishing.
- Show me every change before you make it. Never read or edit .obsidian/plugins/caldera/data.json - it holds my private key.

Projects with live links

What feeds it: "projects" in Caldera/profile.md (Name | status | https://link); commit counts per project from a "projects" map in Caldera/activity.json

Turn it on: List public projects with their live URL; the routine can add commit counts per project.

AI usage (tokens by model and provider)

What feeds it: Claude Code and Codex logs on this computer (read by the plugin itself), or an "ai" block in Caldera/activity.json for more machines and your products' APIs

Turn it on: Automatic on the computer where you code; the routine adds other machines and sources (counts only).

Prompt for your AI
caldera-ai.md
Help me turn on AI usage on my profile on my Caldera page (https://caldera.social/@<my handle>/). Caldera builds that page from my Obsidian vault: <ABSOLUTE PATH TO MY OBSIDIAN VAULT>

AI usage: tokens processed for me, by model and provider (counts only - never prompts, file names or costs).
1. On the computer where I run Obsidian, Caldera already reads Claude Code (~/.claude/projects) and Codex (~/.codex/sessions) logs by itself. If the AI usage section is empty, check those folders exist here.
2. To include other computers or my own products' API usage, write an "ai" block into "<ABSOLUTE PATH TO MY OBSIDIAN VAULT>/Caldera/activity.json" from a scheduled script (no AI call per run): {"total": <tokens in the window>, "window_days": 45, "models": [["Claude Opus 5.5", n]], "providers": [["Anthropic", n]], "sources": [["Coding agents (3 machines)", n], ["Product APIs", n]], "note": "one short public sentence"}, and "tokens" per day in each "days" entry. Keep "prompt_version" and every other key already in that file.

What it knows

What feeds it: "knows" in Caldera/profile.md (Label | vault folder) or a "knows" list in Caldera/activity.json

Turn it on: Pick the folders that describe your knowledge (counts only, never note names).

Inbox to knowledge

What feeds it: an "inbox" block in Caldera/activity.json: {"total", "kinds": [["Reports", 61]], "series": {"label", "weeks": [["Aug 3", 5]]}}

Turn it on: If an agent files your email attachments into the vault, have it count them by kind (counts only).

Every day

Daily journal

What feeds it: caldera_headline / caldera_focus / caldera_wins / caldera_themes in daily notes (YYYY-MM-DD.md)

Turn it on: The setup prompt's routine writes one entry per workday from your notes, calendar, sent email, commits and AI sessions.

Scoreboard (streak, rank, 30-day bars)

What feeds it: notes the plugin counts + the daily counts in Caldera/activity.json

Turn it on: On by default; richer once the routine writes meetings, emails, prompts, commits, docs and tokens per day.

Activity heatmap

What feeds it: notes created and changed per day, plus outside counts from Caldera/activity.json

Turn it on: Automatic; the routine adds calendar, email, AI and code counts.

Growth

What feeds it: notes in the vault over time

Turn it on: Automatic.

The live city

Live visitors on your city

What feeds it: the Caldera snippet (t.js with your site key) on each of your websites

Turn it on: Copy each district's snippet from Settings -> Caldera -> Live traffic (or caldera.social/me) into that site's shared layout.

Prompt for your AI

Help me turn on live visitors on my city on my Caldera page (https://caldera.social/@<my handle>/). Caldera builds that page from my Obsidian vault: <ABSOLUTE PATH TO MY OBSIDIAN VAULT>

1. Get my snippets from Obsidian: Settings -> Caldera -> "Live traffic on your city": one line per district (project), like <script async src="https://caldera.social/t.js" data-site="KEY"></script>. Districts marked private stay "Private" to visitors.
2. For each website I run, find its repo on this computer and the project/district it belongs to (ask me if unsure), and add that district's line to the site's shared layout so every page has it (for example the root layout, _app/_document, base template or index.html head). Don't add it twice.
3. Mark conversions where they happen: call caldera('signup') after a signup and caldera('buy') after a purchase (guard with if (window.caldera)).
4. Deploy each site the way it's normally deployed (ask me first if it goes to production), open the site, then open my city: the visit should appear within a minute.
