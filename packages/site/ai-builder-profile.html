Create an AI builder profile

An AI builder profile on Caldera is a page generated from your GitHub, and optionally your Obsidian vault, that shows what you build with AI: projects and commits, tags worked out from your public repos, AI usage by model, and your coding agents on a living city. Sign up with GitHub and it is ready in about 30 seconds.

Updated 2026-10-18 · by mjonesone, LLC

What goes on it

  • Building tags from public repo topics, descriptions, READMEs and dependencies; add or hide tags yourself.
  • Projects and Code from GitHub.
  • AI usage and a daily journal with the Obsidian plugin.
  • Your own sections such as talks, stack or office hours (modules).

Steps

  1. Sign up with GitHub.
  2. Check your tags under What you're building and edit them.
  3. Follow people in People who build the same things.
Try it free. Sign up with GitHub in about 30 seconds, or install Caldera in Obsidian. Sign up with GitHub Install for Obsidian

Questions

How are tags chosen?

From your public repos; private repos only ever add a language.

Can I edit my tags?

Yes, add or hide them on your building page or in Caldera/profile.md.

Will it list me publicly?

Yes by default in People; you can be unlisted.

Is it free?

Yes.
