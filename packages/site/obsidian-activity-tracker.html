Activity heatmap, streaks and a leaderboard for Obsidian

Caldera adds a GitHub-style 26-week activity heatmap, a daily activity score, streaks, your best days and a friends leaderboard to your Obsidian vault, shown on your public profile. The score combines notes written plus optional outside counts (meetings, emails sent, AI prompts, commits, documents), with caps so a bulk import can't win a day.

Updated 2026-10-18 · by mjonesone, LLC

What's measured

Notes created and changed each day come from the vault itself. Outside activity comes from Caldera/activity.json, which your AI agent or scripts can fill with counts. Only numbers are published.

Compete with friends, positively

The leaderboard ranks you against friends by points this week. There are no downvotes, and points come only from your own activity.

Try it free. Sign up with GitHub in about 30 seconds, or install Caldera in Obsidian. Sign up with GitHub Install for Obsidian

Questions

How is the score calculated?

Weighted daily counts (meetings, emails, prompts, commits, notes, documents), each capped so bulk imports can't dominate. The formula is shown on every page under "How the score works".

Can I hide the heatmap?

Yes. Every section can be switched off in Caldera/profile.md.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. The plugin runs on Obsidian desktop and mobile, and profiles are responsive.
